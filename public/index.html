The short version

I’ve worn the hats you don’t see on a resumé — founder, CTO, lead, engineer — and I keep coming back to one thing: shipping software people pay for.

As co-founder of Aplinode and head of product on Maintaineo, I run an AI-powered asset-management platform end to end — from the QR on the machine to the maintenance history auditors trust. Before that I co-founded and ran Markaaf Studio with a six-person team, led technology as CTO of Turbo Services, and built client products across farmhouses, academies, and exporters.

Today I work where web development meets AI: custom web applications, intelligent automation, and backend systems on Firebase and Supabase. I’m expanding into AI engineering proper — agents, pipelines, and the systems that let a small team do the work of five.
